# Portfolio Analyzer

A personal dashboard that runs on your own laptop and explains your **Groww** stocks and mutual funds in plain English. Every holding gets a **Buy / Hold / Sell** signal (Continue / Review / Switch for funds) with the reasons behind it, and a picture of its strengths and weaknesses. It can also screen NSE stocks for new ideas and sync results to Google Sheets.

> **Signals are generated by rules from public data. This is not investment advice. Consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before acting.**
>
> The app never asks for broker logins and never places trades. Your data comes only from files you export or a Google Sheet you share, and it stays on your laptop.

## What it does

- **Reads your holdings** from a Groww export, a CAS statement, a Google Sheet or manual entry, and works out value, P&L, day change and XIRR.
- **Gives each stock a Score Shape:** a six-sided picture of **Value, Future, Past, Health, Momentum and Dividends**. Each side scores 0–6 from simple pass/fail checks such as "P/E below the sector median" or "Debt within your risk limit", so a bigger, rounder shape means more checks passed. Every check is listed with a tick or a warning, the number behind it and a sentence on why it matters. The strongest ones are pulled out as **Rewards** and **Risks**.
- **Gives each stock a signal** from technical and fundamental rules weighted for your investing style (intraday, swing, long-term…) and risk level, with a stop-loss, targets, a suggested position size and a tax note.
- **Checks your mutual funds** against their benchmark: returns, consistency, risk, cost (Direct vs Regular) and overlap between your funds.
- **Tells you what needs attention:** an **Action needed** list, most severe first, and your portfolio's overall Rewards and Risks.
- **Finds new ideas:** the Screener scores the Nifty 50/100/200/500 with the same rules, and the Watchlist tracks ideas over time. **Compare** puts two to four stocks side by side.
- **Exports** a printable portfolio report (HTML or PDF), CSV files, and Google Sheets tabs.

Every chart has a one-line caption saying what it shows, signals always carry words and icons (not just colour), and there are light and dark themes.

## Install on your laptop

Works on macOS and Linux. On Windows, use the Docker option with [Docker Desktop](https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/).

### Option 1: without Docker (macOS / Linux)

You need [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/) (Python) and Node 20.

1. Install uv: `curl -LsSf https://astral.sh/uv/install.sh | sh`
2. Install Node 20: `curl -o- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvm-sh/nvm/v0.40.3/install.sh | bash && nvm install 20`
3. Download this project: `git clone https://github.com/Tesseract-coder/wealth-mgmt.git && cd wealth-mgmt`
4. Run `make setup`. It installs Python 3.12 and everything else, and creates your `.env` settings file.
5. Run `make dev`, and leave it running.
6. Open **http://localhost:5173** in your browser.

To stop the app, press Ctrl-C in the terminal. To start it again later, run `make dev` in the same folder.

### Option 2: with Docker (macOS, Linux or Windows)

1. Download this project (step 3 above, or **Code → Download ZIP** on GitHub) and open a terminal in its folder.
2. Copy the settings file: `cp .env.example .env` (on Windows: `copy .env.example .env`).
3. Run `docker compose up --build`.
4. Open **http://localhost:5173**.

Your data is kept in Docker's `appdata` volume, so it survives restarts and rebuilds.

### First run

1. Choose your **investing style** and **risk level** (Medium by default). You can change both later in **Settings**.
2. Download your holdings from Groww ([how](docs/GROWW_EXPORT_GUIDE.md)) and drop the file on the **Import** page. To try the app first, use the **fake** example files in `sample_data/`.
3. The first price download starts on its own and takes a few minutes; **Settings → Data refresh** shows progress. Then open **Overview**.
4. Optional: connect Google Sheets ([guide](docs/GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md)).

### Updating

Stop the app, then run `git pull` and `make setup` (or `docker compose up --build`). Your data and settings are kept.

If port 8000 or 5173 is already used by something else, set `BACKEND_PORT` / `FRONTEND_PORT` in `.env`.

## On your phone

Not supported yet. The app runs on your laptop and, to keep your portfolio private, only answers your laptop's own browser, so phones and other devices can't reach it. Use it in a browser on the laptop where it's installed.

## A tour of the app

- **Search:** press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) or **/** anywhere, or click the search button at the top, to jump to any of your stocks or funds, any NSE or BSE stock or mutual fund, or any page.
- **All stocks:** every NSE stock and every share listed only on BSE (about 4,500), with filters for exchange, Nifty index and sector. A stock's data downloads the first time you open it, and a nightly top-up loads 200 more each weeknight, so the list fills in over a few weeks.
- **Overview:** your portfolio's Score Shape (your stocks weighted by value), total value, P&L, day change and XIRR; **Action needed** as cards (click a stock's card for a quick look at its signal, shape and reasons without leaving the page); your Rewards and Risks; allocation and value vs the Nifty 50; and your biggest holdings. If prices are out of date it says which, with a **Refresh prices now** button.
- **Holdings:** every stock as a card (shape, signal, P&L, weight) or as a sortable table. Search, filter by signal, sector or confidence, and sort by any side of the shape. Switch style or risk level to see the same holdings another way. The **Signals**, **Technical**, **Chart patterns** and **Fundamental** tabs list the readings behind every holding; **Chart patterns** ends with a guide to all 15 patterns.
- **A stock's page** reads top to bottom, with a section menu: Summary, Price chart (candlesticks with moving averages, Bollinger bands, support/resistance, RSI, MACD and chart patterns), **Chart patterns** (head and shoulders, double tops and bottoms, rounding bottoms, cup and handle, wedges, flags and pennants, and triangles found on the chart, each drawn on the stock's own bars with what it means, whether it's a buy or a sell signal, which trading style it suits, and the prices that confirm or cancel it), Valuation (price vs an estimated fair value), Future growth, Past performance, Financial health, Momentum, Dividends, Ownership, **What to do** (the signal, reasons, stop-loss, targets, position size, tax note) and **Test the rules** (a backtest). Each section opens with its checks; every technical and fundamental reading is in an expander.
- **Mutual funds:** each fund's shape and signal, overlap between your funds as a heatmap, and for each fund its NAV vs benchmark, rolling returns and worst falls. You can enter expense ratios, and upload AMC portfolio files (Import → Fund portfolios) for overlap.
- **Compare:** two to four stocks with their shapes on one chart, then every check side by side.
- **Screener:** **Run scan** scores a whole index, all NSE stocks, the BSE-only ones or the entire market in the background (the first Nifty 500 scan takes most of an hour, the entire market several hours; later ones only fetch what's changed, and **Cached data only** scores what's already loaded in minutes). Then pick a preset (Top scores, Quality at fair price, Momentum breakout, Pullback in uptrend, Growth, Dividend + value) or build and save your own filters. Each idea shows its shape, entry zone, stop-loss, targets and how it would change your sector weights. **Watch** adds it to the **Watchlist**, which shows what changed since you added it.
- **Import:** shows each step (Choose file → Upload → Check columns → Match tickers → Done) and says how to fix anything that fails.
- **Settings:** profile (with a table of what each risk level changes), thresholds, data refresh, Google Sheets, exports and system status.

Out-of-date or missing data is always labelled with the reason, and the app never guesses a signal it can't support: it says "Not enough data" instead.

### Portfolio report

**Overview → Portfolio report** opens a one-page report with your summary, Action needed, allocation, every stock and fund with its signal and reasons, the top screener ideas and a data-freshness list. Use **Print / Save as PDF** for a PDF (laid out for A4 landscape, with the disclaimer on every page), or **Settings → Export** to download it as HTML.

### Test the rules (backtest)

On a stock's page, **Test the rules** replays the technical signal rules over the last 3 years, using only the prices known on each day, and compares following them with buy-and-hold. Fundamentals have no free point-in-time history so they aren't replayed, and costs and taxes are ignored. **Past results don't predict future returns.**

### Google Sheets

**Settings → Google Sheets** connects one sheet. With a free Google Cloud *service account* (a robot you share the sheet with, not your login), the app imports your holdings from a tab and writes **Summary**, **Analysis_Stocks**, **Analysis_MF** and **Screener** tabs, stamped with the time and the disclaimer, optionally after every daily refresh. It never touches your other tabs. **Test connection** says what to fix. Setup takes about 10 minutes: [docs/GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md](docs/GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md).

## Keeping data up to date

Prices and NAVs come from free sources: Yahoo Finance (delayed, end of day), mfapi.in and AMFI (NAVs) and NSE archives (symbol and index lists). See [docs/DATA_SOURCES.md](docs/DATA_SOURCES.md). Everything is cached on your laptop and labelled **fresh / stale / unavailable**; every page shows when prices were last updated.

While the app is running it refreshes on its own (India time): prices Mon–Fri at 16:00, NAVs daily at 23:30, fundamentals on Saturdays at 10:00. It can't refresh while stopped, so on the next start it catches up on anything stale; **Settings → Data refresh** has a **Refresh now** button and shows the next run of each. Upload Screener.in exports (Import → Screener.in data) for ownership data and 10-year history.

## Settings and configuration

- **Settings → Thresholds** edits risk limits, the buy threshold, signal bands and style weights in the browser. Changes are checked before saving and apply at once; **Reset** goes back to the defaults.
- `backend/config/profiles.yaml` holds every threshold; `backend/config/checks.yaml` holds the Score Shape checks and their wording. Both are checked when the app starts, so a typo stops it with a clear message rather than giving wrong signals.
- `.env` holds ports and paths. A Google service-account key goes in `secrets/` (or upload it in Settings).

[docs/SCORING_METHOD.md](docs/SCORING_METHOD.md) explains every calculation.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Red **Backend offline** banner | The app's server isn't running or is on another port. Run `make dev` and check `BACKEND_PORT` in `.env`. The banner clears itself once it answers. |
| "Something went wrong on the server (…)" | A bug or unexpected data; nothing was changed. The full error is in the terminal running `make dev`. Try again; **Refresh now** under Settings → Data refresh fixes most missing-data cases. Please [send feedback](#share-feedback). |
| Prices marked **stale** or **unavailable** | Yahoo Finance may be busy or down. The app keeps the last good data and retries on the next refresh; **Settings → Data refresh** shows the error. |
| A stock's signal says **Insufficient data** | It has no price history yet (refresh), or its ticker didn't match (fix it on the Import page). |
| The app stops at start-up with a config error | A threshold edit is invalid; the message names it. Fix it, or delete `backend/var/profiles.local.yaml` to drop your threshold edits. |
| Port already in use | Set `BACKEND_PORT` / `FRONTEND_PORT` in `.env`. |
| Google Sheets errors | See [the Sheets guide's troubleshooting](docs/GOOGLE_SHEETS_SETUP.md#troubleshooting). |

## Privacy and safety

- The app runs only on your laptop and answers only your laptop's browser (`127.0.0.1`). Nothing is uploaded except requests to the free data sources (they see only ticker symbols and scheme codes) and, if you set it up, Google Sheets.
- It never asks for broker logins, passwords, PINs, OTPs or TOTP secrets, and it has no code to place orders.
- CAS PDF passwords are used once, in memory, and never stored.
- Your data (`backend/var/`), settings (`.env`) and keys (`secrets/`) are never committed to git.

```
backend/      FastAPI app (app/), config/ (profiles.yaml, checks.yaml), tests/
frontend/     React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind
docs/         Setup guides and the scoring method
sample_data/  FAKE example exports for testing
secrets/      Your Google service-account key (gitignored)
```

- Colours, spacing and type are CSS variables in `frontend/src/styles/tokens.css` (light and dark), used through Tailwind (`bg-surface`, `text-fg-muted`, `text-good`…). Use these rather than raw palette colours; `tokens.test.ts` checks text contrast.
- Shared components are in `frontend/src/components/ui` and `components/analysis` (ScoreShape, CheckList, SectionHeader, RewardsRisks, ChartCaption, SignalPill). Open **http://localhost:5173/dev/components** to see them all with sample data.
- `frontend/scripts/screenshots.sh <dir>` screenshots every page at desktop and phone width in both themes. `docs/screenshots/` is gitignored because the images show your own portfolio.
