# Scoring method

This document explains, in plain English, every number the app computes and how it is read. Each setting named in `code font` lives in `backend/config/profiles.yaml`.

> Signals are generated by rules from public data. This is not investment advice. Consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser before acting.

## Part 1: Technical readings

The technical engine reads a stock's chart on each timeframe of your investing style and produces **observations**. Each observation has:

- **Text** with the actual numbers, e.g. *"RSI(14) 28.4: oversold (at or below 30)"*
- A **direction**: bullish ▲, bearish ▼ or neutral ●
- A **category**: trend, momentum, volatility, volume, structure, relative strength or pattern
- A **minor** flag for weak evidence (candlestick patterns, Bollinger stretch)

Phase 6 turns observations into the technical score (0–100). This phase only reads the chart.

### Timeframes per style

| Style | Timeframes analysed (`styles.*.timeframes`) |
|---|---|
| Intraday / short-term | 15-minute, hourly, daily |
| Swing | daily |
| Positional | daily, weekly |
| Long-term | weekly, monthly |
| Custom | daily |

Periods are counted in bars of the timeframe: "SMA 200" is 200 days on a daily chart and 200 weeks on a weekly one. Weekly and monthly bars are built from daily bars. If the current week or month isn't over yet, the report says its bar is still forming.

### When data is missing

An indicator that doesn't have enough bars isn't calculated. It is listed under **Not available** with the reason, e.g. *"SMA 200 (needs 200 bars, have 60)"*, which is common on monthly charts. Missing values are never filled in or estimated. In Phase 6, missing inputs lower the **confidence** of a signal, not its score.

### How the indicators are calculated

The definitions follow the standard textbook formulas as implemented by TA-Lib. The test suite checks the app's values against TA-Lib's output on a 400-bar reference series (`backend/tests/fixtures/technical_reference.csv`).

| Indicator | Definition |
|---|---|
| SMA *n* | Average of the last *n* closes |
| EMA *n* | Exponential average, α = 2/(n+1), seeded with the SMA of the first *n* closes |
| RSI 14 | Wilder's RSI: 100 × avg gain / (avg gain + avg loss), Wilder-smoothed. Flat prices read as 50 |
| MACD 12/26/9 | EMA12 − EMA26; signal = EMA9 of MACD; histogram = MACD − signal. (TA-Lib seeds its first values slightly differently; the two agree after the first ~100 bars.) |
| Stochastic 14/3/3 | Raw %K = position of the close within the 14-bar high–low range; %K = 3-bar average of that; %D = 3-bar average of %K |
| ROC 20 | % change in the close over 20 bars |
| ADX 14, +DI, −DI | Wilder's directional movement system. ADX measures trend *strength*; +DI vs −DI shows its *direction* |
| Supertrend 10, 3 | A trailing line 3 × ATR(10) from the bar's midpoint. It sits below price in an uptrend and flips when a close crosses it |
| Bollinger 20, 2 | 20-bar SMA ± 2 standard deviations (population). %B = where the close sits between the bands (0 = lower, 1 = upper) |
| ATR 14 | Wilder-smoothed true range (includes gaps from the previous close) |
| Historical volatility 20 | Standard deviation of daily log returns × √252, annualised |
| Volume ratio | Today's volume ÷ average of the *previous* 20 bars |
| OBV | On-balance volume: running total of volume, added on up closes and subtracted on down closes. Its trend is the slope over 20 bars ÷ average volume |
| Delivery % | From NSE's daily bhavcopy: share of traded quantity actually delivered (EQ series only) |

### How each reading is interpreted

| Reading | Bullish ▲ when | Bearish ▼ when | Setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price vs 200 / 50 SMA | Close above | Close below | — |
| 50 vs 200 SMA | Golden cross within the last 10 bars, or 50 above 200 | Death cross, or 50 below 200 | `cross_lookback_bars` |
| EMA 9 vs 21 | 9 above 21 (a fresh cross is called out) | 9 below 21 | `cross_lookback_bars` |
| ADX | ADX ≥ 25 with +DI > −DI | ADX ≥ 25 with −DI > +DI | `adx_trending`, `adx_weak` (below 20 = "no clear trend", neutral) |
| Supertrend | Up | Down | — |
| RSI | ≤ 30 (oversold, likely to bounce) or above 55 (positive momentum) | ≥ 70 (overbought) or below 45 (weak momentum) | `rsi_oversold`, `rsi_overbought`, `rsi_neutral_band` (45–55 = neutral) |
| MACD | Above its signal line | Below its signal line | `cross_lookback_bars` for "fresh" crosses |
| Stochastic | %K ≤ 20 | %K ≥ 80 | `stoch_oversold`, `stoch_overbought` |
| ROC | Positive | Negative | — |
| Bollinger (minor) | Close below the lower band | Close above the upper band | A **squeeze** (narrowest band width in 120 bars) is neutral: "a large move often follows" (`squeeze_lookback_bars`) |
| Volume | ≥ 1.5× average on an up move | ≥ 1.5× average on a down move | `volume_surge` |
| OBV | Rising (or rising while price fell: bullish divergence) | Falling (or falling while price rose: bearish divergence) | `obv_slope_min` (smaller slopes = flat) |
| Delivery % | ≥ 1.2× its 20-session average on an up day | Same on a down day | `delivery_surge`, `periods.delivery_avg` |
| 52-week range | Within 5% of the 52-week high | Within 5% of the 52-week low | `near_52w_pct` |
| Breakout / breakdown | Close clears a resistance level by > 1% in the last 3 bars | Close breaks a support level by > 1% | `support_resistance.*`; "on 1.5× volume" or "without a volume surge (less reliable)" is stated |
| Relative strength | Beat Nifty 50 over 3 / 6 months | Lagged Nifty 50 | `periods.relative_strength` |
| Candles (minor) | Bullish engulfing; hammer after a decline | Bearish engulfing | `candle_doji_body`, `candle_hammer_shadow` (doji = neutral) |

ATR, historical volatility, and the nearest support and resistance are shown as neutral information. Phase 6 uses ATR for stop-losses, and support/resistance for entry and target zones.

### Support and resistance

1. Find **swing highs and lows** in the last 250 bars: a bar whose high (or low) is the strict extreme of the 5 bars either side (`pivot_window`). The newest 5 bars can't be confirmed yet.
2. **Cluster** pivots that lie within 1.5% of each other (`cluster_pct`). A cluster touched at least twice (`min_touches`) becomes a level.
3. The nearest level below the close is **support**; the nearest above is **resistance**. If there is none above, the stock is at the top of its range, and the report says so.
4. A **breakout** is a close through a level that already existed before the move, by more than `breakout_buffer_pct`.

### Relative strength

(1 + stock return) ÷ (1 + Nifty 50 return) − 1, over 63 sessions (3 months) and 126 sessions (6 months), using only the dates both series share.

### Data notes

- Prices are delayed end-of-day data from Yahoo Finance, and only completed sessions are used. Intraday bars (15m/1h) are delayed and limited to about 60 days (15m) or 2 years (1h).
- Delivery % covers NSE's EQ series only. BE/BZ stocks are delivery-only by rule, so their 100% says nothing. Indices and BSE-only stocks have no delivery data.
- Indices are shown in points, not rupees, and have no reliable volume, so volume readings are marked unavailable for them.
